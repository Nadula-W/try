A Letter For You, Kavi

🌸 · · · · 🌸

My darling Kavi,

I've written this a hundred times in my head — trying to find words big enough, warm enough, real enough to tell you what you mean to me. I'm not sure they exist. But I'll try, because you deserve every attempt.

You came into my life on a February day and you never really left — not from my mind, not from my heart. You became home. You became the first person I want to tell everything to, the one whose laugh I hear even when you're not around.

You are so beautiful, Kavi. Not just the way you look — though my god, you are — but the way you care, the way you love, the way you see the world. You make ordinary days feel like something worth writing about.

I love every version of you. The quiet morning version. The laughing-so-hard-you-can't-breathe version. The sleepy, warm, soft version. Every single one.

This little website is just my way of saying: I see you. I choose you. I love you. Today and every day that comes after.

Yours, always & completely 💕
